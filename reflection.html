Personal Reflection

My Career Journey and Professional Aspirations

This reflection discusses my dream company, personal strengths, career goals, and the value I hope to contribute as a future Web Security Developer.

Why Do I Want to Work for Intel?

I chose Intel as my dream company because it is a globally recognized technology company known for innovation, software development, and advanced computing technologies. Intel's commitment to technological advancement, continuous learning, and developing secure digital solutions aligns with my career goal of becoming a Web Security Developer.

My older brother previously worked at Intel as a Software Engineer. Growing up, I admired his dedication, technical skills, and achievements in the technology industry. His experience inspired me to pursue a career in Information Technology and motivated me to continuously improve my technical knowledge and professional skills.

Although my brother worked as a Software Engineer, I am interested in pursuing a career in web security and secure software development. I am particularly interested in protecting web applications, securing user data, and developing reliable digital solutions that help create a safer online environment.

Working at Intel would allow me to contribute to a globally respected technology company while developing my technical expertise, gaining valuable industry experience, and supporting the creation of innovative and secure digital technologies.

What Value Can I Bring to Intel?

I can contribute a combination of technical knowledge, creativity, and a strong willingness to learn. Through my studies in Information Technology (Informatics Media), I have developed experience in web development, multimedia technologies, software development, and cybersecurity concepts.

I have also completed projects involving website development, augmented reality, and multimedia content creation. These experiences have strengthened my problem-solving abilities, teamwork skills, and adaptability when working with technology.

Why Should Intel Hire Me?

Although I am still at the beginning of my professional journey, I am a dedicated learner who is committed to continuously improving my technical skills and industry knowledge. I am responsible, adaptable, and willing to take on challenges that help me grow.

I believe my positive attitude, strong work ethic, and motivation to learn new technologies would enable me to contribute effectively to a professional working environment.

What Skills Make Me Suitable for This Career?

  • HTML, CSS, and JavaScript development skills.
  • Basic knowledge of cybersecurity principles and web security.
  • Problem-solving and analytical thinking abilities.
  • Teamwork and communication skills developed through academic projects.
  • Creativity and multimedia development experience.
  • Version control experience using GitHub.
  • Willingness to continuously learn and improve.

My Strengths and Weaknesses

Strengths

  • Creative and innovative thinking.
  • Strong teamwork and collaboration skills.
  • Good communication abilities.
  • Adaptability and willingness to learn.
  • Strong interest in technology and cybersecurity.

Weaknesses

  • Limited real-world industry experience.
  • Still developing advanced cybersecurity skills.
  • Need more practical experience with professional security tools.

To address these weaknesses, I am actively completing cybersecurity courses, studying web security concepts, and building projects that strengthen both my technical and practical skills.

Why Would a Company Choose to Hire Me?

A company may choose to hire me because I combine technical knowledge, creativity, teamwork, and a strong commitment to continuous learning. I am motivated to improve my skills, adapt to new technologies, and contribute positively to organizational goals.

As I continue developing my expertise in web development and cybersecurity, I am committed to becoming a professional who contributes to creating secure, reliable, and innovative digital solutions while supporting organizational success.
